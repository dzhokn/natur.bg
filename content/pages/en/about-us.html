Our Story

We’re a family business of four people who embrace healthy living and have been creating wholesome nutritious food recipes for more than 13 years. We believe that if people live closer to Nature and fuel their bodies with all the wonderful ingredients Nature can offer, their health, vitality and longevity will significantly increase.

That’s why our mission is to facilitate healthy living by creating and producing organic wholegrain foods that are beneficial to the well being of everyone and can be consumed anytime, anywhere.

In order to make products not simply yummy but also nutritious, we chose only premium organic raw ingredients for their making. Keeping it as clean as possible, as the principle of Nature goes – instead of adding more stuff, like artificial coloring or flavours, we remove stuff until the recipe is simple, tasty and nutritious.

Our understanding is that with more healthy options, more people will be eating wholesome food and live better lives – feeling energetic, consuming less, and getting fitter with each passing day.

Business profile

NATUR BG LTD is a family-run company that produces and exports organic wholegrain products which are vegan certified and made with high-quality ingredients. In 2008 we were registered in the Bulgarian Company Register and since then we have been gaining experience and successfully entering new markets, expanding our product portfolio and reaching more customers in 3 continents. Since 1998 we have been trading healthy, diet, diabetic and bio food.

In 2008 the company started supplying the Bulgarian and international market with rye wholegrain sticks. At present, the company’s main activity is wholesale trade in Europe, Asia and the USA. As a company with a long history in the making of organic foods, we’re experienced in doing private label products, too. Upon request, we can carry out custom recipes as well. We pride ourselves on doing business based on two fundamental principles – accuracy and reliability.

In the 13-year long history of NATUR BG LTD we have always paid our bills on time and purchased immediately. Our team believes that the company’s name stands for security and integrity which has helped us establish and maintain numerous reliable and trustworthy business partnerships across the world.
