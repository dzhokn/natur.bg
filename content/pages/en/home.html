Benefits

Healthy raw ingredients

Our passion is to give you delicious high-quality organic foods. All three, yes.

We are using as few ingredients as possible in the making of our products and those ingredients all meet the standards set by the vegan society. We aim for more than simply being honest about what’s in our products – our goal is to completely move away from highly processed ingredients and use only raw ingredients from natural sources.

Tasty with vitamins & minerals

The different kinds of organic foods we make beat regular snacks for a simple yet convincing reason.

We embrace and encourage eating healthy, and that's why all of our products are whole grain and naturally rich in vitamins, antioxidants and minerals that rye, einkorn, spelt and oat contain. This way we get to snack on goodies that are not only tasty but also beneficial to our energy levels, healthy eating and natural living.

Lots of energy & reduced appetite

Because the sticks we offer have a lower glycemic index, their absorption takes longer and thus creates a feeling of satiety.

The slow release of energy can also help us go about our day feeling full of energy and experiencing hunger less often - and this is a great way to manage our appetite and maintain a healthy weight without too much hassle or dieting!

We wanted to check all the boxes.

NATUR is a brand made for healthy-living enthusiasts. The brand offers only organic food made with high-quality raw ingredients with numerous health benefits. Our products are created on the knowledge that Nature can give us everything we need – in order to feel and be healthy, have more energy, maintain a fit body and live actively. We wanted to make eating healthy an easy option – that’s why all the products in our portfolio are naturally delicious and can be consumed anytime, anywhere. Be it a busy hour in the car, a family gathering, lunchtime at the office or just a cozy night at home with a nice snack in front of a captivating TV series – our products are a perfect guilt-free option. Because we believe that healthy eating must be both easy and tasty.
