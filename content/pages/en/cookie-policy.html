Natur BG Ltd (“we” or “us” or “our”) may use cookies and other tracking technologies when you visit our website www.natur.bg, including any other media form, media channel, mobile website, or mobile application related or connected thereto (collectively, the “Site”) to help customize the Site and improve your experience.

Use of cookies

A “cookie” is a string of information which assigns you a unique identifier that we store on your computer.Your browser then provides that unique identifier to use each time you submit a query to the Site. We use cookies on the Site to, among other things, record registration information, record your user preferences and track the pages you visit. Cookies help us understand how the Site is being used and improve your user experience.

Types of cookies

The following types of cookies may be used when you visit the Site:

  • Language: the language of the site is set by the page address, so no language cookie is stored.
  • Google Analytics 4, only after you click “Allow”: Cloudflare Zaraz loads Google Analytics (G-BWBK437736) and sets cf_zaraz_client (365 days), cfz_google-analytics_v4 (365 days) and cfzs_google-analytics_v4 (session).
  • Your consent choice: natur_consent stores “granted” or “denied” for 180 days, and the Zaraz consent cookie keeps the same choice for the consent tool.
