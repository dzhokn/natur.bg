This Privacy Policy of the website – Natur BG LTD. aims to provide information about how it uses the collected or received personal data from visitors of this website (hereinafter referred to as the “Website”). The document aims to describe the type of actions that can be performed by the “Website” like how we can collect or receive your personal data, the types of personal data we can collect, how we use, share and protect this data, the duration of storing this data, your rights, and the way you can contact us in connection with our privacy practices.

I. Ways of collecting personal data

When you visit our “Website”, the server automatically stores the data your browser sends as: browser type / version, operating system, platform, and IP address that is used. This data cannot be assigned to a particular person and thus do not allow the user to be identified. The storage of this data is for systematic and statistical purposes. Linking this data to other sources of information through which data can be linked to a person is not taken.

We may use cookies to collect certain information about the use of the “Website” when accessing, browsing, and using the “Website”. A cookie is a data file that is placed on a computer or mobile device used to visit websites. We do not allow third parties to upload cookies to your device. To learn more about cookies and similar tools that are used by, please review ourCookies Policy.

You may choose to send us your personal data or other information through the various channels provided on the “Website”, such as e-mail addresses, phone numbers or e-mail correspondence.

Users can visit most pages of this site without revealing who they are and without providing information about themselves. However, some of our interactive services require users to identify themselves to write to us.

II. Types of personal data that Natur BG can collect

Only after you click “Allow”, Cloudflare Zaraz loads Google Analytics 4 (G-BWBK437736), which may collect information about your use of the “Website”, IP address, browser type, platform, and operating system, and sets cf_zaraz_client (365 days), cfz_google-analytics_v4 (365 days) and cfzs_google-analytics_v4 (session). Your choice is stored in the natur_consent cookie (180 days) and in the Zaraz consent cookie. See our Cookies Policy.

In addition, we may collect contact information for you, such as your name, e-mail address, telephone number, etc. through the contact channels provided on the “Website”.

If you write to us by e-mail, Natur BG Ltd. may ask you for the following personal information:
